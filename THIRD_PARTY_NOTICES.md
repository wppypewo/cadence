[py-fsrs](https://github.com/open-spaced-repetition/py-fsrs), and `tests/fixtures/fsrs-golden.json`

## Facts taken from Wiktionnaire

`lexicon/genders.json` records grammatical gender and elision facts looked up on
[fr.wiktionary](https://fr.wiktionary.org/) (CC BY-SA 4.0). No definition or translation text from Wiktionnaire
is stored in this repository; the Chinese glosses in `lexicon/glosses.json` and the spelling corrections in
`lexicon/spelling.json` were written for this project.
See [lexicon/README.md](lexicon/README.md).

## Not in this repository

The card bank is built at build time from five external sources that are not distributed here, including
Verbiste (GPL-2.0-or-later) conjugation tables and FLELex (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0). Build output goes to the
git-ignored `data/bank/`. See the "Data and licensing" section of the README.
